2. HARDWARE, BATTERY & THERMAL SAFETY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
ThermalPulse interacts directly with Windows power plans, standard GPU
management libraries (NVML / ADL2), and firmware-exposed WMI thermal
and fan interfaces. By installing and using this software, you
acknowledge that:

- High power draw on battery accelerates Li-Ion battery wear and can
  cause voltage-sag shutdowns at low charge levels. Keep the built-in
  Battery Safety Cutoff (20% or higher; 30% recommended) enabled.
- Custom or manual fan curves override factory fan behavior while the
  app is running. Keep the Thermal Fail-Safe enabled at all times.
- On battery power, discrete GPUs remain subject to firmware-enforced
  VBIOS DC wattage limits.
- Exiting ThermalPulse cleanly restores fan control to Auto and unlocks
  any active software GPU clock cap.
